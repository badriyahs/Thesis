Houses of the Moon. November 14, 2026, Sharjah Museum of Islamic Civilization.

Before the clock and the map, the night sky was the time and space infrastructure everyone shared. Reading it was a communal, local knowledge. Across Arabia, people read the movement of the stars as a map, a compass, a calendar, and a clock, to find their way, to know the season, to wait for the rain, and to tell the hour of the night. In Houses of the Moon, Badriyah Alsalem talks about her journey in understanding the desert sky, and what is lost today, now that we’ve stopped looking up.
