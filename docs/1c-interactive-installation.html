Names of the Sun:
Interactive Installation

Diriyah Art Futures (DAF), Riyadh, 2026.

Exhibition

Continuum, September 4 – November 14, 2026

Materials

Frosted acrylic, brushed stainless steel

Projection

Tracks the sun in real time, shifting continuously as it moves across the sky

Sound

Ney and duduk — the ney carries the sun from sunrise to noon, the duduk from noon to sunset. After dark, the full cycle repeats on a loop.
